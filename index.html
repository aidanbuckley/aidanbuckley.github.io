About

Background

Before college, I built and ran Kicks Aidan, a sneaker resale business that generated over $60,000 in revenue across 350+ transactions and 400+ wholesale accounts. By late 2022, I recognized margin compression signaling a market decline and exited deliberately, closing at peak seasonal revenue. That decision, knowing when to hold and when to walk away, led me to Fordham and to investing.

At Fordham's Gabelli School of Business, I'm pursuing a Finance major with a concentration in Alternative Investments. I serve on the executive board of Fordham's AI Club and am a member of the Alternative Investments Club's VC Group. From April to June 2026, I wrote startup funding and venture capital coverage for Vesting, a media platform with 500K+ followers across TikTok, X, Instagram, Twitch, and YouTube. Since June 2026, I've been a Content Intern at OneSixOne Ventures, producing a founder interview series and running outreach to investors and operators across the ecosystem. Alongside that, I've spent the past year building a real investment framework through deliverables produced for competitive programs at actual funds.

My long-term goal is venture capital, with a secondary ambition to start a company. Both paths demand the same foundational understanding: what makes a startup actually work. I'm building that understanding from the operator and investor side at the same time.
